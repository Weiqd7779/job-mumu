# Community evidence policy

Use community evidence to calibrate interpretation, not to count market demand.

A supported community signal normally requires:

- At least 3 independent discussions.
- At least 2 sources.
- At least 1 firsthand interview or work account.
- Preference for material from the last 3 years.

For every signal, report:

- Number of discussions reviewed.
- Source names and dates.
- Number of firsthand accounts.
- Whether it is role-general or company-specific.
- Supporting and conflicting claims.
- Confidence.

If the threshold is not met, state that evidence is insufficient. Never pad the count with duplicate reposts or search snippets.
